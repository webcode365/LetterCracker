Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

Last updated: January 1, 2025  |  Effective: January 1, 2025

LetterCracker ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website lettercracker.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit LetterCracker, our systems and third-party services may automatically collect certain technical information including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, referring URLs, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This information is collected to understand site traffic, improve user experience, and deliver relevant advertising.

Information You Provide

If you choose to submit our optional lead capture form, you may provide your name, email address, and phone number. This information is collected voluntarily and used to send you word game tips, updates about LetterCracker, and relevant offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser cookies and localStorage to remember your theme preference, search history, game high scores, and whether you have accepted our cookie policy. We also use third-party cookies from advertising partners (see Section 4). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the LetterCracker website and tools
  • To personalize your experience (e.g., remembering your preferred theme)
  • To send periodic emails if you have subscribed (you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • To show relevant advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners
  • To analyze website traffic and usage patterns via Google Analytics
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

LetterCracker uses the following third-party services that may collect data about you:

  • Google AdSense — Displays advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your interests. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com.
  • Google Analytics — Tracks website usage statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Amazon Associates — We participate in the Amazon affiliate program. Clicking Amazon links may use cookies. See Amazon's Privacy Policy for details.
  • ipapi.co — Used to detect your approximate location and IP address for display in the visitor bar. No personal data is stored by us from this service.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting google.com/settings/ads. We comply with all Google AdSense program policies.

5. Data Retention

Information you provide through our contact or lead forms is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, or until you request deletion. Technical log data is retained for up to 12 months. Cookie and localStorage data persists until cleared by your browser.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including: access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, and the right to opt out of marketing communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lettercracker.com.

7. Children's Privacy

LetterCracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page or email privacy@lettercracker.com.
