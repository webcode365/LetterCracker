Why Your Starting Word Determines Everything

In Wordle, your opening word is the most important decision you make. A well-chosen starting word gives you maximum information about which letters are in the answer, which positions are correct, and which letters to eliminate entirely. Players who master their opening strategy solve the puzzle in fewer guesses on average.

The Science Behind High-Value Starting Words

The best Wordle starting words share three key characteristics: they contain the most commonly used letters in the English language, they avoid repeating any letter, and they cover a spread of vowels. Letters like E, A, R, O, T, I, and S appear most frequently across all 5-letter words, making them the highest-value letters to test first.

Top 10 Best Starting Words for Wordle

Based on letter frequency analysis of every valid Wordle answer, these are the most statistically powerful starting words you can use:

CRANE — Contains C, R, A, N, E. Covers four of the top ten most common letters. Consistently ranked among the top three starting words by data analysis.

SLATE — Contains S, L, A, T, E. The S is especially valuable since many English words end in S, and this combination tests a strong spread of consonants.

AUDIO — Contains four vowels in one word. If you prefer to eliminate all major vowels first, AUDIO is your best first guess, leaving consonant patterns clear for your second word.

RAISE — Contains R, A, I, S, E. Five of the most common letters in 5-letter words, with zero letter repetition. A consistently high-performing opener.

STARE — Contains S, T, A, R, E. The T and R combination is extremely common in English, and this word tests them both in common positions.

AROSE — Heavy on vowels with strong consonants. Useful when you want to lock down vowel positions quickly.

STORE — Contains S, T, O, R, E. The ST cluster at the start tests one of the most frequent English consonant pairings.

SNARE — Contains S, N, A, R, E. The N is particularly valuable as it appears in a large proportion of Wordle answers that other top words do not test.

IRATE — Contains I, R, A, T, E. Four vowels and only one consonant, but R and T are both high-frequency, making this a strong vowel-focused opener.

TRACE — Contains T, R, A, C, E. Similar to CRANE in coverage, the C tests an additional high-value letter that CRANE misses.

Two-Word Opening Strategy

Many expert Wordle players use a fixed two-word opening strategy to eliminate the maximum number of letters before guessing. Popular two-word combos include CRANE + SPLIT, which together test C, R, A, N, E, S, P, L, I, T — ten of the most common English letters in just two guesses, leaving you three attempts to identify the exact answer.

What to Avoid in Your Opening Word

Avoid starting with words that contain uncommon letters like Q, Z, X, J, or V. These letters appear in very few Wordle answers, so using them in your first guess wastes a guess entirely. Similarly, avoid words with repeated letters as your first guess — you want to test as many different letters as possible in each turn.

Final Tips for Consistent Wordle Success

Consistency is key in Wordle. Pick one or two starting words and commit to them — tracking patterns over time helps you build intuition for common letter positions. Remember that Wordle draws from a specific curated word list, which means unusual or rare words are unlikely to appear as answers. Focus your strategy on common everyday English words and you will find your average guess count dropping within a week of consistent practice.
