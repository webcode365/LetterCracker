Our Story

LetterCracker was born out of frustration with slow, cluttered, and outdated word tools that disrupted the flow of gameplay. We are a passionate team of developers and avid word game enthusiasts who wanted something faster, smarter, and beautifully designed — a tool we would actually want to use ourselves in the middle of a game.

What started as a weekend project quickly evolved into a full-featured word game utility suite, now trusted by thousands of players every day from over 80 countries around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make every word game more enjoyable by giving players instant access to the best possible word tools — completely free, always. Whether you are a competitive Scrabble tournament player, a daily Wordle devotee, or someone who just loves word puzzles, LetterCracker has the right tool for you.

What We Offer

  • Word Unscrambler — Find every valid word from any set of letters in milliseconds.
  • Anagram Solver — Discover all hidden anagrams for crosswords and puzzles.
  • Wordle Solver — Crack any Wordle in three guesses or fewer.
  • Dictionary Checker — Instantly verify any word across multiple official dictionaries.
  • Random Word Generator — Generate words for games, writing, and education.
  • Word Scramble Game — Challenge yourself with a timed word scramble game.

Why LetterCracker?

Unlike other word tools that charge premium fees or bombard you with ads, LetterCracker is designed to be lightning fast, beautifully minimal, and completely free. We run entirely in your browser — no server round trips, no sign-ups, no tracking. Just results.
