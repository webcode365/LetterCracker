What Is an Anagram?

An anagram is a word or phrase formed by rearranging all the letters of another word or phrase, using each original letter exactly once. For example, the letters in LISTEN can be rearranged to spell SILENT, ENLIST, and TINSEL — three completely different words sharing the same set of letters.

Famous Anagram Examples

  • LISTEN → SILENT, ENLIST, TINSEL, INLETS
  • EARTH → HEART, HATER, RATHE, THARE
  • ANGEL → GLEAN, LANGE, LEGAN, GALEN
  • RACE → CARE, ACRE, ACER, ARCE
  • STAR → ARTS, RATS, TARS
