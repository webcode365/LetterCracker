Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This policy explains how LetterCracker uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help websites remember preferences and understand usage patterns.

How We Use Cookies

Essential Cookies — Store your theme preference, cookie consent status, and game scores. Required for site functionality.

Analytics Cookies — Google Analytics collects anonymous traffic data. Opt out at Google Analytics Opt-out.

Advertising Cookies — Google AdSense and Amazon Associates use cookies to serve relevant ads and track affiliate conversions.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. To manage ad personalization visit Google Ad Settings.

LocalStorage

We also use browser localStorage to store your preferences (theme, history, scores). This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
