Cookie Policy

How LetterCracker uses cookies and similar technologies

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This policy explains how LetterCracker (lettercracker.info) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use their services, improving your experience on return visits.

How We Use Cookies

Essential Cookies — These are required for the website to function correctly. They store your theme preference (dark/light/ocean), cookie consent status, game scores, and session data. Without these cookies, certain features of LetterCracker will not work.

Analytics Cookies — We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site, including pages visited, time spent on each page, and general geographic location. This data helps us improve our tools and content. You can opt out at any time via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising Cookies — Google AdSense and Amazon Associates use cookies to serve relevant advertisements and track affiliate conversions. These cookies may collect data about your browsing habits across websites to serve personalized ads.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our site are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous traffic measurement
  • Google AdSense — advertising personalisation and delivery
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate conversion tracking
  • Google Fonts — font delivery (no personal data collected)

Managing Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of LetterCracker. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies from being set

To manage ad personalisation, visit Google Ad Settings.

localStorage

In addition to cookies, LetterCracker uses browser localStorage to store your preferences such as theme choice, search history, and game high scores. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear localStorage at any time through your browser's developer tools or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookie Consent

By continuing to use LetterCracker, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. A consent banner is displayed on your first visit. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies and not continuing to use the site.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
