What Is a Word Unscrambler?

A word unscrambler is an online tool that rearranges a set of jumbled letters to reveal every valid word hidden within them. LetterCracker supports inputs of up to 45 letters — covering even the longest words in the English language. Our high-speed engine checks an extensive dictionary of valid words, returning results sorted by length and Scrabble point value so you always find the highest-scoring play.

Which Word Games Does LetterCracker Support?

LetterCracker is purpose-built for every major word game, including:

  • Scrabble (US & Canada) — Uses the NWL / OSPD dictionary for tournament-legal words.
  • Scrabble (UK & International) — Supports the full CSW / SOWPODS word list.
  • Words With Friends — Optimized with the ENABLE word list for accurate results.
  • Wordle — Use our dedicated Wordle Solver to crack any daily puzzle.
  • Wordscapes & Word Cookies — Filter by exact word length to match grid constraints.
  • Crossword Puzzles — Use prefix and suffix filters to nail down specific positions.

How to Get the Best Results

  • Enter all your rack letters — the more letters, the more valid words we find.
  • Use ? or * for each blank tile on your rack.
  • Open Advanced Options and apply Starts With or Ends With filters for specific board positions.
  • Sort by Scrabble Points to instantly find your highest-value play of the game.
  • Click any word chip to copy it directly to your clipboard.

Why Players Trust LetterCracker
