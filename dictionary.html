Why Check a Word in the Dictionary?

Whether you're in the middle of a heated Scrabble game or wondering if a new slang word has made it into the official lexicon, our Dictionary Checker gives you an instant, authoritative answer. We compare words against over 170,000 entries drawn from official Scrabble word lists (NWL, CSW) and the ENABLE word list used by Words with Friends.

Which Dictionary Does LetterCracker Use?

Our database combines multiple official word lists: the NWL (North American Scrabble), CSW/SOWPODS (International Scrabble), and ENABLE (Words with Friends). If a word is found in any of these lists, it will show as valid in our checker.
