How to Use the Wordle Solver

Type one letter into each cell of the grid. After entering a letter, click the cell to cycle through its color state: white (not yet used), green (correct letter in the correct position), yellow (letter is in the word but in the wrong spot), and grey (letter is not in the word at all). Once you have entered all your clues, click Find Solutions to see every remaining valid 5-letter word.

Wordle Strategy Tips

  • Start with words that use common letters: E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, R
  • Use your second guess to eliminate as many letters as possible
  • Avoid reusing grey letters — they waste valuable information
  • Yellow letters must appear somewhere in your next guess, just not in the same position
  • If you are down to 1–2 guesses, use LetterCracker to find the safest remaining option
