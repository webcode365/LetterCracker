How to Use the Wordle Solver

Type one letter into each cell of the grid. After entering a letter, click the cell to cycle through its color: white (unused), green (correct position), yellow (wrong position), and grey (not in word). Once all clues are entered, click Find Solutions to see every remaining valid 5-letter word.

Wordle Strategy Tips

  • Start with words that use common letters: E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, R
  • Use your second guess to eliminate as many letters as possible
  • Avoid reusing grey letters — they waste valuable information
  • Yellow letters must appear somewhere in your next guess, just not the same position
  • If you are down to 1–2 guesses, use LetterCracker to find the safest remaining option
