How to Play Word Scramble

A scrambled word appears on screen. Type the correct unscrambled word and press Enter before the 60-second timer runs out. The faster and more accurately you answer, the higher your score. Use the Hint button if you are stuck, and Skip to move on without penalty. Try to build a streak for bonus points!

Scoring System

  • All words — scored by real Scrabble point value of each letter
  • Rare letters (Q, Z, X, J) — worth extra points
  • Streak bonus — get 3 in a row for a speed boost!
