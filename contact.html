Frequently Asked Questions

Is LetterCracker completely free? +
Yes — 100% free, always. No subscription, no hidden fees, no sign-up required. Use every tool on our site without paying a cent.
Which dictionaries do you support? +
We support NWL (North American Scrabble), CSW/SOWPODS (International Scrabble), and ENABLE (Words with Friends / standard English). Select your preferred dictionary in the Advanced Options panel.
How accurate are the word results? +
Very accurate. Our word database is sourced from official tournament-approved word lists and is updated regularly. If you find a word that should be included or excluded, please let us know!
Can I use LetterCracker for competitive Scrabble? +
LetterCracker is intended as a learning and practice tool. Use it to study valid words, improve your vocabulary, and explore strategies. Always follow the rules of your specific game or tournament.
